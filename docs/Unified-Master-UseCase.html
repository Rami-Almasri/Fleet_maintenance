The Rental Lifecycle The operational backbone

🛡️ Gatekeeper — Readiness Gate (Pre-Rental test)

No car is handed over until it passes the 9-check readiness validation: availability, registration/insurance valid through the return date, no open safety-critical fault, service window, GPS, last check-in closed, cleaning. Blockers stop the booking; warnings must be acknowledged.

📷 Handover — 16-item photo check + Customer Signature/OTP

At Check-Out the driver captures the full mandatory photo set (front/rear/sides/corners/rims/glass/interior/odometer/fuel/pre-existing scratches/key/spare + 360° video for luxury). The system refuses to complete the handover until coverage is met and the customer confirms condition by signature or OTP. Every photo is tied to contract + vehicle + time + location + driver.

🚦 The "Rental-First" Rule

Booking is permitted for Green and Orange — Orange requires a mandatory, recorded Condition Acknowledgment by the sales agent. Red is a hard block. Utilization is maximised; safety stays a hard barrier.

Resolved (Rev. 7): Yellow is officially Rentable-with-Acknowledgement — treated exactly like Orange; the gate never blocks it but logs a yellow_grade_ack at contract creation.
🔄 Check-In / Scratch Flow the accountability chain

Driver documents (photo + flags a new scratch on the diagram) → the system auto-creates a Damage Evaluation draft (pending_assessment) → a notification is sent to Abdullah & Walid (Managers) for final assessment. The driver has no pricing or liability authority — they only mark. Nothing marked can be silently dropped.

Lifecycle: Readiness Gate → Handover (photos + signature) → Out on Rent → Check-In (photos + scratch flags) → auto Damage-Eval draft → 🔔 Managers assess.
2

Maintenance & Incident Engine

🔧 Routine maintenance

Oil / filter / battery driven by mileage tracking and service-due detectors. Battery adds health + replacement-interval tracking. Config-driven protocols (config/inspector_protocols.php).

🔁 Stability Cycle Engine (post-accident) — strict & enforced

When a Post-Accident car goes Under-Repair → Available, the engine injects a stability_check_ticket every 5 days. It cannot be closed by a click — a close needs 3 fresh photos (Odometer + Engine Bay + Tires) + an explicit Pass. Miss the deadline by even 1 hour → an instant Safety Block (highest priority) + Admin alert. After 3 clean cycles (15 days) it auto-graduates. Every check is logged in auto_maintenance_audits.

🪜 Support Ladder (Incident Gate)

A fault reported mid-rental escalates: 1) Phone support (remote) → 2) On-site repair (Abu Marouf visits) → 3) Vehicle swap if the first two fail. Each rung is logged on the incident ticket (resolution_path).

💰 Approval Matrix

Damage repairs require financial approval that links the manager's assessment to the Maintenance Order: a garage quotation, then tiered approval by amount (Supervisor → Fleet/Ops Manager → Branch Manager → GM), with luxury cars and accidents bumped a tier. No work starts and no car moves to the garage until the right person approves.

Damage → Repair: Manager confirms assessment + cost → vehicle auto In-Repair → task in Abu Marouf's queue → quotation → tiered approval → repair → QA → release.

2.5

Customer & Money Protection The cash-flow & risk layer

🧾 Deposit Ledger

Track debits / credits per contract on a real ledger. The manager's assigned damage cost, fines, and fuel post against it; the lifecycle is hold → charge → release/refund. Refund balance can flow to the customer wallet.

🚧 Fine / Toll Hold

On Check-In the contract enters pending_charges; the system blocks any deposit refund until Salik / traffic fines are cleared post-return. A landed fine draws down the deposit; the hold auto-clears after the configured window or on manual settle.

⚠️ Customer Risk

A customer-level flag: blacklist → block booking at the Readiness Gate; high-risk → require a larger deposit; watch → warn. Feeds directly into Pillar 1's gate.

CC

Command Center Cross-cutting transparency layer

📡 Real-time Activity Feed

Every critical action (valuation, approval, set-to-ready, inspection/damage filing, override) is pushed to a global stream showing actor · timestamp · the exact change. Lean: one thin activity_log table. No more silent updates.

📍 Vehicle Live-Status Tracker

Each car shows status → owner/assignee (Driver / Garage / Dispatcher); if in Maintenance → which garage + which supervisor. Reads livePosition() + logistics + garage stint — no new table.

📊 Management Overview (Omar + Admin)

Yellow-in-field · pending approvals (waiting on the hierarchy) · stale tasks (claimed but unfinished past X hours, red) · Vehicles in Stability Cycle + next check due — all read from the state machine.

I&E

Intelligence & Enforcement Phases 9–11

🏭 System-chosen garage (multi-criteria) + 💰 Cost Intelligence

The routing engine scores every garage on 10 signals (specialty, fault-type, vehicle-class, rating, comeback-rate, completion-time, price, parts, proximity, warranty) → top 3 with reasons, and — from history — an estimated price. A quote >20% over the historical average raises a High-Cost Warning (non-blocking). Each Close writes actual_cost and sharpens the garage_cost_stats snapshot.

🚨 Accident-to-Garage Chain (receipted)

New-damage/accident → instant high-priority, non-dismissible ping to Abdullah & Walid (photos + liability) → Submit-Assessment → auto note-carrying task to Abu Marouf, who can't see the ticket until assessed. Every stage stamps notified_at / viewed_at / actioned_at.

📏 Usage Monitoring & Mileage Enforcement

Every status change requires an odometer photo + entry; a lower-than-previous reading is a Data Integrity Error, blocked until the Admin (Admin-only) approves. Distance over the contractual allowance +5% auto-tags excess_mileage + alerts the Admin; a 🟢/🟡/🔴 Usage Dashboard tracks each rental. GPS/geo-fence deferred until a live feed is connected.

5 · Logic check — one open item left

Of the three contradictions flagged earlier, two are now resolved (Rev. 7 decisions). One remains — a single decision before build.

1✅ RESOLVEDYellow grade defined

Decision: Yellow is officially Rentable-with-Acknowledgement, treated exactly like Orange. The gate does NOT block a Yellow car, but MUST log an explicit yellow_grade_ack at contract creation.

Status: already live in the shipped booking gate — Yellow already requires the recorded acknowledgment (condition_ack_grade = 'yellow' is the ack record). No further work.
2✅ RESOLVEDValuation ≠ Approval

Decision: the manager's role is to valuate/assess (set cost + scope); approval is strictly governed by the Approval Matrix (by amount). valuated_by (the manager) and approved_by (the authorized signatory) are separate.

Logic: when the valuated cost exceeds the manager's own tier limit, the ticket transitions to pending_approval_hierarchy and routes up; within his limit he is also the signatory. Grounding (In-Repair) may happen at valuation, but garage work waits for approved_by.
3Abu Marouf repairs AND signs off his own QA (separation of duties)

Pillar 3 has the Inspector (Abu Marouf) doing field repairs and QA / "Ready for Release" sign-off. That means the person who fixes the car also passes it — which breaks the independent QC we already built (the re-inspection layer deliberately sends a failed repair to a different person). Omar's own protocol says "any car without an independent QA Pass cannot return to rental."

Decision needed: either (a) a second person (a Manager or a Fleet-Ready officer) signs the final QA on repairs Abu Marouf executed, OR (b) accept self-QA for minor jobs and require independent QA only for safety-critical repairs. Recommended: (b) — independent QA mandatory for safety-critical, self-QA allowed otherwise.
