A · Booking & Eligibility

8 scenarios
A1Clean booking — Green carworks today (gate)
Actor:SalesTrigger: new rental request
  1. Sales picks vehicle + dates → the Readiness Gate runs the 9 checks.
  2. All checks pass (Green grade, docs valid through return, no open fault).
  3. Handover proceeds to Check-Out.
Outcome: Booking allowed → car goes to handover.
A2Blocked — Red gradeworks today
Actor:SalesTrigger: booking on a Red-graded car
  1. Sales selects a car graded Red (critical / grounded).
  2. The gate refuses — front-end and back-end both reject.
Outcome: Hard block. Pick another car or send it to maintenance.
A3Orange / Yellow — condition acknowledgmentworks today
Actor:SalesTrigger: booking on an Orange/Yellow car
  1. Sales selects an Orange (cosmetic) or Yellow (needs service) car.
  2. A mandatory acknowledgment prompt fires: confirm the customer was told.
  3. Agent confirms → the acknowledgment is recorded on the contract (condition_ack_*).
Outcome: Booking allowed with a recorded acknowledgment (Rental-First).
A4Blocked — docs expire before returnplanned
Actor:SalesTrigger: registration/insurance lapses inside the rental window
  1. The gate compares registration + insurance expiry to the return date (not today).
  2. They lapse before the car comes back.
Outcome: Hard block until documents are renewed.
A5Blocked — open safety-critical faultplanned
Actor:SalesTrigger: car has an open brakes/steering/tyre fault
  1. An open maintenance order alone does not block (Rental-First).
  2. But the fault is on the hard-coded safety list → the gate blocks.
Outcome: Hard block — safety is the only non-negotiable barrier.
A6Blocked — blacklisted customerplanned
Actor:SalesTrigger: customer risk flag = blacklist
  1. The gate reads the customer's risk level.
  2. Blacklist → booking refused.
Outcome: Hard block on the customer, regardless of car.
A7High-risk customer — bigger depositplanned
Actor:SalesTrigger: customer risk flag = high-deposit
  1. The gate flags the customer as high-risk.
  2. A larger deposit is required before handover.
Outcome: Booking allowed with a higher deposit.
A8Readiness warning — service / GPS / cleaningplanned
Actor:SalesTrigger: soft check flags amber
  1. Service due soon, GPS silent, or cleaning not done → warning (not a block).
  2. Agent acknowledges each; a manager can clear it.
Outcome: Booking allowed after acknowledging the warnings.

  2. System raises "deposit ready to release."
Outcome: Deposit refunded (to wallet or cash) — balance settled.

F · Maintenance Engine & Stability Cycle

6 scenarios
F1Routine — oil / filter dueworks today
Actor:SystemTrigger: mileage crosses the interval
  1. Service-due detector fires from odometer tracking.
  2. The car surfaces in the maintenance forecast / alerts.
Outcome: Routine service scheduled before breakdown.
F2Battery dueplanned
Actor:SystemTrigger: battery age / health interval
  1. Battery interval or health check crosses threshold.
  2. A "battery due" alert is raised.
Outcome: Battery replaced proactively.
F3Stability Cycle — auto-triggeredplanned
Actor:System → Abu MaroufTrigger: a Post-Accident car goes Under-Repair → Available
  1. The engine detects the transition on a Post-Accident-flagged car.
  2. stability:scan injects a stability_check_ticket into Abu Marouf's queue — repeated every 5 days.
  3. Each generated check is logged in auto_maintenance_audits with its due date.
Outcome: Rebuilt car is enforced onto a 5-day cycle — no toggle, no memory.
F4Strict Gate — can't close by a clickplanned
Actor:Abu MaroufTrigger: tries to close a stability ticket
  1. He taps close without the required evidence.
  2. Server rejects it — a close needs Odometer + Engine Bay + Tires photos and an explicit Pass.
Outcome: Close blocked until all 3 photos + a Pass are supplied — no skip.
F5Missed by 1 hour → Safety Blockplanned
Actors:SystemAdminTrigger: 5-day deadline lapses
  1. The hourly enforcement pass sees next_stability_due < now.
  2. Auto-applies safety_block (highest priority — overrides Available/Rented) and logs it to auto_maintenance_audits with late_by_minutes.
  3. Instant alert to the Admin.
Outcome: Car grounded the moment it's even 1 hour late; the Admin knows immediately.
F6Graduation — 3 clean cyclesplanned
Actor:SystemTrigger: 3 consecutive clean cycles (15 days)
  1. The car passes 3 consecutive stability checks (a fail/miss would reset the counter).
  2. The engine auto-graduates it.
Outcome: Moved off the Stability Cycle onto the Standard Maintenance Schedule.

I · Safety-Decision Audit (the Safety Guardian)

3 scenarios
I1Agreement — silent passplanned
Actor:Abu MaroufTrigger: inspector writes a description
  1. He types "brake noise" → the system scans keywords → suggests 🔴 Safety-Critical.
  2. He marks the car Non-Rentable — his call agrees with the suggestion.
Outcome: No conflict — saved silently, no audit, no alert.
I2Trivial issue grounded — reverse conflictplanned
Actors:Abu MaroufAdminTrigger: low-severity keyword, non-rentable call
  1. He types "minor paint scratch" → system suggests 🟢 Minor.
  2. He marks the car Non-Rentable — contradicts the suggestion (grounding a trivial car).
  3. System flags audit_required, forces an override reason, alerts the Admin.
Outcome: Recorded — the Admin can see why a rentable car was pulled off the line.
I3Brake fault cleared as rentable → Admin alertedplanned
Actors:Abu MaroufAdminTrigger: high-severity keyword, rentable call
  1. He types "brakes soft" → system suggests 🔴 Safety-Critical.
  2. He marks the car Rentable anyway — a dangerous car cleared against system logic.
  3. Save is blocked until he writes an override reason.
  4. An instant push hits the Admin; the decision lands in the audit queue with the full before/after trail.
Outcome: His judgment is honored, but nothing dangerous is cleared invisibly — the guardian is on.

K · Intelligence & Enforcement (Phases 9–11)

6 scenarios
K1The system picks the garage (multi-criteria)planned
Actor:System → SupervisorTrigger: a ticket needs a garage
  1. The routing engine scores every garage on specialty · fault-type · vehicle-class · rating · comeback-rate · completion-time · price · parts · proximity · warranty.
  2. It returns the top 3 with each score + reasons + an estimated price from history (Phase 9).
  3. The supervisor accepts the pick, or overrides with a written reason.
Outcome: The best-fit garage is chosen on data, not relationships — and any override is logged.
K2High-cost variance → cost warningplanned
Actors:SystemAdminTrigger: a garage quote is >20% over history
  1. The predictor compares the quote to garage_cost_stats for that model + fault.
  2. It's more than 20% above the historical average → a High-Cost Warning badge.
Outcome: You see you're likely overpaying — non-blocking: a premium garage is still allowed.
K3Accident → receipted chain to managersplanned
Actors:SystemManagersTrigger: damage_status = NEW / accident event
  1. Instant High-Priority Push + In-App to Abdullah & Walid with photos + liability field (non-dismissible).
  2. Their Submit Assessment auto-drops a note-carrying task to Abu Marouf — who couldn't see it before.
  3. notified_at / viewed_at / actioned_at logged per role.
Outcome: A guaranteed, receipted hand-off — "I didn't get the notification" is impossible.
K4Odometer rollback → Data Integrity Errorplanned
Actors:DriverAdminTrigger: entered odometer < previous record
  1. At a status change, the entry is lower than the last logged reading.
  2. The save is hard-blocked with a Data Integrity Error.
  3. Only the Admin can approve the correction (supervisors cannot).
Outcome: Odometer tampering can't slip through — the transaction stops until the Admin clears it.
K5GPS/Odometer discrepancy → tamper flagplanned · needs GPS feed
Actor:SystemTrigger: GPS distance diverges from odometer distance
  1. The engine compares GPS-calculated km to the odometer-logged km.
  2. A large divergence raises a tamper / GPS-gap flag for review.
Outcome: The odometer stays the record of truth; GPS corroborates and catches manipulation. (Activates when the GPS feed is connected.)
K6Excess mileage → Policy Violation alertplanned
Actors:SystemAdminTrigger: active km > contractual allowed + 5%
  1. Allowed = miles_allowed_pd × rental days; the rental crosses allowed + 5%.
  2. System auto-tags the rental excess_mileage and fires a Policy Violation Alert to the Admin; the Usage Dashboard turns 🔴.
Outcome: Contract abuse is caught automatically — no manual mileage auditing.
