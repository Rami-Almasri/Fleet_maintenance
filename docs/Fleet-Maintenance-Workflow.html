Fleet Operations · Process Re-engineering

Fleet Maintenance Workflow

Replacing the manual, WhatsApp-driven maintenance process with a single self-documenting workflow — where every action by the Inspector, Logistics, and Controllers automatically generates the data, in real time.

Why we are changing

The current process lives in chat messages. Nothing is tracked, searchable, or accountable.

⚠️ Today — WhatsApp & spreadsheets

  • Status lives in scattered chat messages
  • Controllers re-type everything into a spreadsheet by hand
  • No record of who did what, or when
  • Cars get “lost” between inspection and the garage
  • No alerts — problems are noticed late

✅ Proposed — one live workflow

  • Each role acts in the app; the system records it automatically
  • Controllers get a live dashboard — no manual entry
  • Every step is time-stamped and attributed
  • The car’s exact state is always visible
  • Real-time notifications hand the car between teams
2

The three roles & their use cases

A single “Maintenance Ticket” moves between roles. Each handoff is triggered by a real action — and instantly notifies the next team.

🔍

Abu Maroof

Inspector — starts & closes every job
Role 1
UC-1Trigger a maintenance job

Selects a vehicle and chooses the reason — Periodic Maintenance or Customer-Reported Issue — then submits a test-drive report (notes, photos, damage flags) directly into the system.

System →Opens a Maintenance Ticket, captures the report once, and automatically alerts the Logistics team. No message to send.
UC-2Re-inspect on return

When the car comes back, reviews it with a before/after comparison and marks it Passed or Failed.

System →Pass returns the car to the available fleet; Fail re-opens a repair leg. A car can never silently re-enter service unrepaired.
🚚

Logistics / Delivery Team

Moves the car to the garage & relays feedback
Role 2
UC-3Receive the job in real time

Gets an instant notification the moment Abu Maroof submits his report — no need to watch a chat.

System →Push + in-app bell, deep-linked straight to the car’s ticket.
UC-4Scan / photograph the odometer

Captures the mileage at hand-off by photographing the odometer.

System →Stores the reading and photo, and feeds the existing mileage-tracking checks automatically.
UC-5Select the destination garage

Picks the garage from a pre-defined list — no free typing, no spelling mistakes.

System →Records exactly where the car went; controllers see it live.
UC-6Update with garage feedback

Logs the garage’s intake notes on hand-off.

System →Moves the car to “Under Repair” and opens the repair record automatically.
📊

Lin & Marwa

Controllers — oversee the whole fleet, live
Role 3
UC-7Live lifecycle dashboard

A board — not a spreadsheet — that tracks every vehicle in real time across Inspection Pending → In Transit → Under Repair → Ready for Re-inspection, showing the car, reason, garage, mileage, and how long it has been in each stage.

System →Updates itself from the live tickets. Zero manual data entry.
UC-8Oversee & unblock

Spots cars stuck too long in a stage, re-assigns garages, approves repair bills over the threshold, and cancels false alarms.

System →Flags overdue repairs and pending approvals automatically — nothing slips.

User stories

The same workflow, told from each person’s point of view.

Inspector
As an Inspector, I want to start a job by picking a car and a reason, so the right context follows the car all the way through.
Inspector
As an Inspector, I want to submit my test-drive report in the app, so my findings are recorded once and seen by everyone — not retyped from a chat.
Inspector
As an Inspector, I want submitting my report to alert Logistics automatically, so I never have to chase anyone to move the car.
Inspector
As an Inspector, I want to re-inspect and pass/fail the car on return, so nothing goes back into service unrepaired.
Logistics
As Logistics, I want a real-time alert when an inspection is submitted, so I can act without watching a chat.
Logistics
As Logistics, I want to photograph the odometer in the app, so the mileage at hand-off is captured accurately.
Logistics
As Logistics, I want to pick the garage from a fixed list, so the controllers always know exactly where the car went.
Logistics
As Logistics, I want to log the garage feedback, so the car officially moves to “Under Repair” and the record opens itself.
Controllers
As a Controller, I want a live board of every car’s state, so I replace my spreadsheet with a screen that updates itself.
Controllers
As a Controller, I want to see how long each car has sat in a stage, so I can chase stalls before they become problems.
Controllers
As a Controller, I want alerts for overdue repairs and bills needing approval, so nothing slips through.
Controllers
As a Controller, I want every change stamped with who and when, so I have an audit trail without scrolling chat history.
